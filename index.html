About

Donghyun Jeong is a builder and researcher exploring how AI reshapes business and the way we think. He works across three domains — AI, community, and startups — turning emerging technology into products people can actually use.

He is the co-founder of D.one and a Computer Science student at Incheon National University. His work spans self-supervised representation learning for RGB–thermal imagery, diffusion-based virtual try-on, and speech-separation systems for accessibility.

At the center of his work is D.one, a community for solo founders. Most founders don't quit for lack of ability — they quit because the stretch before the first traction is lonely. D.one is where they push through it together: working solo, but never alone.
